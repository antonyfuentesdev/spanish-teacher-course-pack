Level exam A2 · Forms 1, 2, 3 · Group 2 (listening, speaking) about 55 min; Group 1 (reading, writing) about 65 min · Rules: 13.9 §3, 13.11 Gates §5–§7, and the Administration and scoring guide on 13.13

<b>Scope (13.1 A2 topics and 13.11 A2 can-do):</b> life experiences (pretérito perfecto); shopping, sizes, comparing and paying (lo / la / los / las, comparatives); a past trip or event with dates (pretérito indefinido, regular + ser/ir, tener, hacer, estar); travel plans (ir a, pensar, me gustaría + infinitive); simple opinions (creo que, me parece que, para mí); feelings and reactions (gustar-type verbs: encantar, molestar, preocupar, interesar).

<b>Instructions to the learner are in English at A2</b> (the bot reads them). Listening items have at most 3 short options, read twice, or need a short answer; the learner may ask once per item to hear the options again. Clips are slow to normal speed, each played twice.

## Speaking anchors (A2)

| Criterion | 5 (A2) | 3 (A2) | 1 (A2) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Task achievement | All task points covered; the story has when, where, what and a reaction | About half the points; story lacks time or reaction | Barely attempted |
| Coherence and fluency | Short connected talk with primero, después, luego, porque, pero; normal A2 pauses | Lists of sentences, long pauses, recovers | Isolated phrases |
| Range | Uses present, perfecto, indefinido and ir a as the task needs; topic words for shopping, travel, feelings | Mostly present tense; repeats words | Memorized phrases |
| Accuracy | Indefinido of regular verbs and ser/ir/tener/hacer mostly right; perfecto with he/has/ha; gustar-type agreement mostly right | Frequent tense or agreement errors, meaning clear | Errors block meaning |
| Interaction | Answers, asks follow-up questions, reacts (¡Qué bien!, ¡Qué pena!) | Answers but rarely asks or reacts | Needs constant support |

Sample 5 for Accuracy: "El año pasado fui a Cartagena con mi esposa. Hizo mucho calor, pero nos encantó la comida." Sample 3: "El año pasado voy a Cartagena con mi esposa. Hace mucho calor, nos encanta la comida." (tense errors, meaning clear)

| Criterion | 5 | 3 | 1 |

### Speaking (25 points, 8 min preparation + about 12 min)
