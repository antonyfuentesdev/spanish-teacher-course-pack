Level exam B1 · Forms 1, 2, 3 · Group 2 (listening, speaking) about 65 min; Group 1 (reading, writing) about 90 min · Rules: 13.9 §3, 13.11 Gates §5–§7, and the Administration and scoring guide on 13.13

<b>Scope (13.1 B1 topics and 13.11 B1 can-do):</b> past narration with background and events (imperfecto, indefinido vs imperfecto, antes era… ahora es…); travel, renting, health and services (imperative, conditional for polite requests); asking others to do things (quiero que / necesito que / le pido que + subjunctive); describing people, places and things (relative clauses que, donde, quien; ser vs estar); advice, recommendations and wishes (es importante que, te recomiendo que, ojalá + subjunctive); por vs para.

<b>Instructions are in Spanish from B1</b>, read slowly, and repeated in English on request (13.8). Listening at normal speed, each clip played twice; 3 options per item, or a short answer.

## Speaking anchors (B1)

| Criterion | 5 (B1) | 3 (B1) | 1 (B1) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Task achievement | All task points covered with detail and reasons; requests and advice suited to the situation | Most points covered, little detail | Barely attempted |
| Coherence and fluency | Connected narration and explanation (cuando, mientras, entonces, por eso, al final); some pauses to plan | Linear, some breakdowns, recovers | Phrases, frequent breakdowns |
| Range | Indefinido and imperfecto, imperative, conditional, subjunctive after quiero que / te recomiendo que, relatives | Mostly present and indefinido; avoids the subjunctive | A2-like |
| Accuracy | Past aspect mostly right; subjunctive after the taught triggers mostly right; ser/estar mostly right | Frequent errors in aspect or subjunctive, meaning clear | Errors in basic forms block meaning |
| Interaction | Starts, asks for clarification, makes and answers requests naturally | Responds, rarely starts | Needs constant support |

Sample 5 for Range/Accuracy: "Cuando era niño, vivía en un pueblo donde todos se conocían. Un día, mientras jugábamos en el río, mi hermano se cayó y tuvimos que llevarlo al médico." Sample 3: "Cuando fui niño, vivía en un pueblo. Un día jugábamos en el río y mi hermano se caía, tuvimos que llevarlo al médico."

## Writing anchors (B1)

| Criterion | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Task achievement | All points, right register (formal email or informal message), within the word range | Most points, or register uneven | Off task or under half the range |
| Organization | Paragraphs, clear order, varied connectors (además, sin embargo, por eso, al final) | Ordered, few connectors | Unordered |
| Range | Past aspect, conditional, subjunctive after the taught triggers, relatives, por/para | Repetitive, simple structures | A2-like |
| Accuracy | Mostly correct; errors don't affect meaning | Frequent errors, meaning clear | Meaning often unclear |

Two tasks; the writing-task score is the mean of the two task totals (/20), rounded down.
