How review items come back. Combines Pimsleur's graduated recall inside a session with spaced review across sessions, sized to fit the time actually available.

## Where the queue lives

- In the <b>progress file</b> on the bot's own computer (13.11), in its <b>Review queue</b> table: one row per item with its own due date. Built-in Grok Bot features only; no third-party tool. Overview: "Where progress lives" in the Learning plan overview
- Columns (schema v2, 13.11): ID, Item, Type, Level, Topic, Created, Step, Due, Last result, Miss count (error items keep the corrected form in Item, e.g. "quiero que usted llama → llame"). Retired items move to <code>progress-archive.md</code>
- The bot's memory holds only the next review date and a one-line summary (e.g. "12 items due Thu"), not the queue
- <b>Format:</b> a plain table in the file, not SQLite or a spreadsheet: it needs no installed software. The live queue holds only active items; about 2,400 items by C1 are spread between the queue and the archive
- <b>Before each session</b> the bot re-reads the queue to pick due items; it does not rely on memory for the list. If the file can't be read during a live call (13.11 design assumption A1), the due items come from the next session brief posted in chat
- <b>When it changes:</b> after every call, the bot updates the queue from the call transcript: new items added at step 1, reviewed items moved up or down (Grading below), due dates recalculated
- <b>Design assumptions (no test step):</b> whether memory or the file is usable during a live call, what triggers the after-call write, and whether the file survives a computer reset are all covered by built-in fallbacks: next session brief, daily reconcile and daily backup (13.11 A1–A3). Recall inside a call (below) works on that call's own items and needs no storage

## Item types

- Words and chunks (active list only; passive-list words are heard, not drilled)
- Grammar frames (e.g. quiero que + subjunctive)
- Irregular forms (tuvo, puesto)
- Sounds (perception items)
- The learner's own errors (stored with the corrected version)

## Intervals

<b>Inside a call</b> (Pimsleur pattern, counted in turns, not seconds): a new item comes back after about 1, 3, 8 and 15 exchanges.

<b>Across sessions:</b>

| Step | Next review |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 day |
| 2 | 3 days |
| 3 | 7 days |
| 4 | 21 days |
| 5 | 60 days |
| Done | Retired (counts as "learned"); moved to the archive and still sampled in retention sets and 30-day checks (below) |

Each item is reviewed about 5 times in total, so <b>weekly reviews ≈ new active items per week × 5</b> once the queue matures (plus misses).

## Out-loud retrieval

- The bot gives a cue, never the answer: an English prompt (A1–A2), a situation ("You want the landlord to come today"), or a Spanish definition (B2–C1)
- Learner thinks and answers out loud; turn-holding rules in 13.8
- Sounds: bot says one of a minimal pair, learner says which
- Errors: bot recreates the context that caused the error
- Time per item: about 15–20 seconds

## Grading

| Result | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Correct, quick | Up one step |
| Correct after a hint | Same step |
| Missed at step 1–3 | Back to step 1; asked again later in the same call |
| Missed at step 4–5 | Down two steps |
| Missed 3 times in total | Problem item: 2-minute mini-lesson in the next session (13.2, 13.8) |

## Load per study day and per week (fits 2 h × 3 days as well as daily study)

| Level | New active items per lesson session | Most new items on one day | Weekly review budget | In each 30-min lesson | Review block or call |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1–A2 | 4–5 | 10 | 168 (7 × 24) | 8 (3 min) | rest of the day's share (about 15–20 s per item) |
| B1–C1 | 5–6 | 10 | 210 (7 × 30) | 8 (3 min) | rest of the day's share |

- <b>Study days:</b> the days in the Profile study plan. Due dates snap to study days: "1 day" = the next study day; "3 days" = the second study day after; "7 days" = the same weekday next week; 21 and 60 days = calendar days, moved forward to the next study day. A daily learner has every day as a study day
- <b>Per study day share</b> = weekly budget ÷ planned study days (e.g. 168 ÷ 3 = 56 reviews, about 15–19 minutes, which fits sitting block 3 in 13.2)
- New active items per study day = up to 5 (6 at B1+) per lesson session, at most 2 lesson sessions, never more than 10 a day. At 3 study days a week that is up to 30 new items a week, about 150 reviews a week once mature, inside the budget
- <b>If the learner skips review blocks:</b> only the 8 in-lesson slots are used, so new active items drop to 2 per lesson session. The topic is not extended: its active target is lowered to what was actually introduced (for example 20 instead of 30–40) and recorded in the Profile field "Lowered active targets" (13.1 Vocabulary targets)
- <b>Overdue rule:</b> if overdue items exceed one study day's share, new active items drop to 2 per lesson session (not zero, so every lesson still brings something new) until overdue falls below half a share. The bot says how long catching up takes, from about 17 seconds per item: "You have 60 words to catch up on: about 17 minutes of review. Shall we do it now? We'll still learn two new things today." (B1+: same in Spanish)
- Oldest overdue and problem items come first; missed study days don't pile up: the backlog is spread over the next study days within the share

## Retention after promotion

- Retired items (Done) move to <code>progress-archive.md</code> and are still sampled: the level exam's <b>exam memory check</b> (the retention set: 10 items of the level created at least 21 days earlier, 7/10 needed) and the <b>one-month check</b> 30 days after each level pass (6/10; under that, a refresher before the next exam) (13.9 §3, 13.11 Gates §5 and §7)
- Items missed in a retention set or check go back into the queue at step 1
